# Community event waste: fictional practice notes

These notes are invented for the UAX Studio tutorial. They are not research findings or evidence about a real event. Keep this label in any output that uses them.

## Project question

What should a community group learn before choosing an approach to reduce waste at its events?

## Note A: organizer interview

Source: fictional interview with an event organizer. Date not recorded.

The organizer says volunteers have sometimes found mixed items in the waste bins. The organizer suggests clearer signs, but no counts, photographs, or measurements accompany this account.

## Note B: volunteer interview

Source: fictional interview with a volunteer. Date not recorded.

The volunteer suggests reusable service items. The group has not established storage space, washing capacity, purchasing costs, or the number of people who would participate.

## Note C: planning discussion

Source: fictional planning note. Date not recorded.

Participants suggest comparing clearer bin signs with reusable service items. They want a short brief to decide what questions to investigate next. They have not selected an intervention or agreed on a budget.

## Information that is missing

- A measured waste baseline and a definition of what would count as improvement.
- Attendance estimates for a representative event.
- A confirmed budget and available volunteer time.
- Operational requirements for each option.
- Any measured impact or comparison of the suggested approaches.

## Requested treatment

Preserve the distinction between an observation reported in a fictional interview, a suggestion, an assumption, and missing information. Do not calculate effects, savings, or percentages from these notes. The purpose is to practice structuring a careful evidence brief, not to make a real-world recommendation.
